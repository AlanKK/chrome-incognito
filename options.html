Chrome Incognito Permission

Checking...

For any Chrome extension to open or manage Incognito tabs, Chrome requires the user to explicitly grant permission in settings.

How to enable "Allow in Incognito":

  1. Open a new tab and go to chrome://extensions
  2. Locate Incognito URL Router and click Details
  3. Scroll down and toggle "Allow in incognito" to ON
